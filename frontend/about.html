THE PROBLEM

Can customers trust the weight?

Weighing machines are used every day in shops, markets and businesses. But customers often have no simple way to know whether an instrument has been officially inspected and verified.

This lack of transparency can create uncertainty between businesses and customers.

Is this instrument verified?

Our system provides a simple answer.

Yes, it is verified.

OUR SOLUTION

Turning verification into digital trust.

We connect businesses, verification officers and customers through one simple digital system.

For Businesses

Register weighing instruments and submit verification requests digitally.

For Officers

Inspect instruments, verify their details and approve valid instruments.

For Customers

Scan the QR code attached to an instrument and instantly check its verification status.

HOW IT WORKS

From registration to verification.

The complete verification journey in a few simple steps.

01

Shop / Business Registers

The business registers its weighing machine on the platform by providing the required instrument details.

02

Verification Request

The business submits a request for the weighing instrument to be inspected.

03

Officer Checks the Instrument

A verification officer inspects the instrument and checks the submitted details.

04

Instrument is Verified

Once the instrument passes the required inspection, its status is marked as verified.

05

Certificate is Generated

A digital verification certificate is generated for the approved instrument.

06

QR Code is Attached

A unique QR code is linked to the verified instrument and attached to the machine.

07

Customer Scans the QR

Customers can scan the QR code using their smartphone to check the instrument's status.
